## Live SERP observations

- `basement oil tank removal` is the best newly found removal opportunity. Results include a UK contractor guide, a thin general-information page, Reddit discussions, and a regional Rhode Island contractor. It does not look locked by ten strong U.S. national pages.
- `heating oil tank removal` also has mixed U.S./UK results and a blend of guides, contractors, and forums. A U.S.-specific page can compete, although Angi and established niche sites are present.
- `oil tank replacement cost` has much larger demand but stronger competition from HomeAdvisor, Angi, and HomeGuide.
- `oil tank decommissioning` is geographically mixed. U.S. demand is concentrated in places such as Washington and Oregon, while the broad SERP also contains UK commercial-tank results. It needs location-specific pages rather than one generic national lead page.

## Revenue-model constraint

The existing platform offer is specifically for homeowners requesting oil-tank removal. Replacement, oil-to-gas conversion, pumping, inspection, and remediation traffic must not be counted as payable removal calls until the platform confirms those intents qualify or a separate buyer exists.

`Oil tank replacement` is real residential demand: the live SERP is led by HomeAdvisor, Angi, and HomeGuide cost pages. Replacement normally includes old-tank removal, so it is strategically adjacent, but it is not automatically an accepted removal lead.

## Revised conclusion

The site is not as demand-starved as the first pass suggested. It can support a broader **residential heating-oil tank lifecycle** content architecture:

1. Removal and disposal.
2. Basement and above-ground removal.
3. Replacement and replacement cost.
4. Decommissioning and abandonment.
5. Inspection/sweep and soil testing.
6. Oil-to-gas or heat-pump conversion with old-tank removal.

However, strict removal-only traffic still does not safely guarantee 100 organic clicks per day. The replacement cluster materially increases total addressable search demand, but it requires a replacement/HVAC lead buyer or explicit platform approval. For a $25 payout and $1,000 monthly target, the commercial plan should treat removal as the first monetized offer and replacement as the largest expansion opportunity—not silently mix unqualified calls into the removal campaign.
