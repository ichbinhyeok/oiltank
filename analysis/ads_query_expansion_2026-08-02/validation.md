# Validation Report

## Overall Assessment: Share with caveats

## Methodology Review

The decision is which residential heating-oil query cluster offers the best next SEO leverage after the verified chart library. Demand and advertiser intensity use the reviewed U.S. Google Ads exports for July 2025 through June 2026. SERP composition was reviewed on August 2, 2026. Current route coverage was checked against `ProductRoute.java`.

## Issues Found

1. **Medium:** Google Ads bands are broad and close variants overlap. Keyword counts describe breadth; they are not additive market size.
2. **Medium:** SERP composition is a directional snapshot, not a quantitative difficulty score or ranking forecast.
3. **High for revenue claims:** No accepted-lead conversion data exists for installation, replacement, repair, or cleaning intent.
4. **Low for the recommendation:** The HTML report packager has a reproducible 1440px horizontal-overflow verifier failure, including on an earlier control artifact. The canonical JSON and analysis remain inspectable.

## Required Caveats for Stakeholders

- Installation is a relative honeypot hypothesis, not a guaranteed ranking.
- Replacement remains the larger raw demand cluster.
- Search Console impressions, positions, and clicks should replace SERP priors after 8–12 weeks.
- Lead-buyer acceptance must be established before counting non-removal traffic toward the $1,000 monthly goal.
