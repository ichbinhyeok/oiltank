## Implementation contract

Visual thesis: an uncluttered green/white working surface, with the document question and one useful action visible before explanatory detail.
Content plan: situation → immediate scoped answer → document-gap checklist → region-specific official paths → deeper evidence/request guidance. Home presents document checking and known-record lookup as distinct entry paths.
Interaction thesis: focused input/selection states, explicit checklist generation and visible reset/copy feedback; preserve existing sticky reading navigation and reduced-motion support. No decorative motion or fabricated risk score.

The checker uses self-reported document categories only, not address, email, uploads or free text. No network request, persistence, URL parameters, automatic request submission or claim of document authentication. Output is a research checklist, not a compliance certificate, environmental opinion, tank-free finding or buy/sell recommendation. Region guidance is specifically supported for NJ, NY, OR and WA; other locations use generic custody guidance and the existing catalog's actual coverage. No-JavaScript visitors retain crawlable answers, document-role descriptions and official routes.
